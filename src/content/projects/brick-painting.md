Created for Not Only | Cape Town Market at Texas, this 80 × 100 cm textured
scenic painting takes inspiration from the venue's distinctive pink-brick
courtyard and its place as a meeting point within Cape Town's creative
community.

Using theatrical scenic techniques, I recreated layers of exposed brick, worn
plaster, peeling paint and weathered surfaces to explore the traces that people
and time leave on a space. Sun-faded pinks and earthy neutrals are layered with
aged greens, blues and browns, drawing from both the architecture of Texas and
the broader textures of Cape Town.

Like a fragment taken from a wall you might pass every day, the work reflects a
city continuously built upon and reshaped, its surfaces holding traces of the
people, stories and creative exchanges that have moved through it.
