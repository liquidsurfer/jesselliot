For the first edition of Senses Studio's outdoor event, I worked
collaboratively on an immersive floating canopy installation, contributing to
the hanging of fabrics, sourcing, fabrication and styling of the environment.
My work included hand made collages set in frames, sourced objects such as
mirrors suspended amongst the trees, glass bottles and potions, antique
furniture, beaded canopy details, and custom matchbox gifts created for
attendees.

The project was centred around creating an intimate, sensory environment that
felt considered in every detail. Working alongside an all-female installation
team made the process particularly special, with the collaborative nature of
the build reflecting the softness, care and feminine energy at the heart of
Senses.

> Senses was born as an ode to the feminine. From the very beginning, the
> intention was to create an environment that feels warm, safe, held, and
> deeply intentional. A space where softness is not mistaken for weakness, and
> where people can arrive as they are and feel supported by the space, the
> music, and each other.
>
> What I've come to realise is that when something is created with clear
> intention, it attracts the same in return. Our community shows up with that
> same care and openness, and you can feel it instantly on the dance floor —
> warmth that can't be manufactured. A feeling of being held by the collective.
>
> Senses will always be about more than a party. It's about intention,
> community, and remembering that there is space for tenderness within rave
> culture.
>
> — Words by Tali Azaken, Founder of Senses Design Studio
