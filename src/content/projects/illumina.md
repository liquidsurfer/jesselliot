Illumina, an exhibition of personal works presented under my artist alias at
Tomorrow's Monday event in Cape Town. The collection brought together digital
paintings, graphic works and imagery developed through my wider illustration
and tattoo practice, exploring abstraction, symmetry, dreamlike figures and
recurring symbolic forms.

The name Illumina, drawn from the translation "to illuminate", informed the
physical presentation of the work. Prints were arranged between suspended
frames hanging on chains with the help from the Tomorrow's Monday team and an
altar-like installation of hand-turned wooden stands, candles and sand,
extending the imagery beyond the page and into the surrounding space.

Moving between texture, gentle gradients, repetition and more figurative
compositions, the works explore an intuitive inner landscape, using dreamlike
images and symbols to give form to experiences that can be difficult to
articulate through language.
