For the past three years, I have been part of the team continuously building and
evolving **The Nest** at Waking Life Festival. Working predominantly with
eucalyptus bark and materials gathered from the surrounding land, we weave a
human-scale environment that grows and changes with each edition. My
contribution spans weaving, décor, fabrication, sewing, scenic painting and
lighting installations.

Within the scale and intensity of a festival, The Nest exists as a place to
retreat — to rest, connect, breathe, draw, sleep, or simply tap out in order to
tap back in. Woven amongst sand and winding pathways, bamboo, eucalyptus and
mimosa form shaded spaces layered with Persian rugs, mobiles, moss, mushrooms
and handmade lamps.

The Nest feels less like a constructed installation and more like a living
extension of its surroundings. Working with nature and using natural materials
with very little purchased is how I foresee the evolution of stage design in our
near future.
