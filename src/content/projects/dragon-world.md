Created for the launch of House of 333 a new collection by [Lapis and the Red
Thread](https://lapisandtheredthread.com/collections/dragon-fire-like-water),
This installation Dragon World: Fire like water translated the collection's
mythology into a physical environment.

Conceptualised collaboratively with Alicia Chenard, the space was built from
natural and found materials. Large pieces of bark formed a fragmented,
cave-like landscape, interwoven with ferns, water, suspended metal mesh, smoke
and incense. Pieces from the collection were embedded throughout the structure
like small artefacts, emerging from cracks, hanging between branches and
partially hidden within the landscape.

Rooted in the symbolism of the dragon, the installation moves through the
essence of fire and water, destruction and becoming, strength and softness.
Rather than displaying the jewellery separately from its story, the intention
was to build the world it belonged to, an environment that could be entered,
discovered and experienced.

Medium — found bark, ferns and foliage, water, mesh, metal, smoke, incense,
jewellery and light.
