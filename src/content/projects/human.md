Human is a BBC documentary series exploring 300,000 years of human evolution,
reconstructing different periods, environments and ways of life throughout our
shared history.

Working within the Art Department, my role moved between sourcing, set
dressing, fabrication and scenic painting. We recreated early human
environments using natural and weathered materials, weaving eucalyptus mats,
ageing and dyeing hessian, textiles and animal skins and constructing shelters
from grasses, branches, wool and fur.

A particularly intricate part of my work on this production involved scenically
replicating an existing landscape at miniature scale for in-camera effects. I
painted and dressed miniature rock landscapes, transforming sculpted foam into
detailed replicas of the surrounding Cape mountains by matching their
formations, surface textures, colour and indigenous vegetation to integrate
into the foreground shots. I was also tasked to scenic a large-scale Egyptian
pharaoh sculpture, building layers of colour, texture and weathering to
reference the material qualities of ancient Egyptian statuary.

The production was an exercise in recreating worlds across vastly different
periods of human history, using material, texture and detail to make
constructed environments feel grounded within the landscapes around them.
