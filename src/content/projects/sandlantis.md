A forgotten city lost beneath the sand and aged by time, **Sandlantis** is an
immersive stage environment built around the mythology of a civilisation
surviving above the Earth's last reservoir of water.

I had the opportunity to contribute to Sandlantis across two editions, using
scenic painting, décor and spatial detailing to bring this fictional world to
life. Raw structures were transformed into weathered remnants of a city,
layered with rust, ageing, erosion and traces of human occupation.

Beyond the stage itself, Sandlantis was designed as a world to explore. Hidden
rooms, secret bookshelves, terraces and interactive structures encouraged
people to move through and inhabit the space rather than simply observe it. My
work extended into these interiors, painting and dressing individual spaces to
deepen the sense of discovery and create an environment that felt lived-in and
all-encompassing.

At its core, the project explored resilience, community and our relationship
with natural resources, using world-building and immersive design to imagine
how life might continue within an increasingly hostile landscape.
