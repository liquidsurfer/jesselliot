London Calling is an action-comedy following a hitman forced into hiding after
a job goes wrong. Working within the Art Department, my role spanned prop
sourcing, set dressing and scenic work across a wide range of contemporary
environments.

Unlike the period productions I had previously worked on, this project involved
building spaces around the personalities and lives of individual characters —
from apartments and bedrooms to bars, hotels and other practical locations.
Many sets were constructed from the ground up specifically to accommodate stunt
sequences, meaning the environments needed to feel convincing on camera while
being designed around the action and, in some cases, their eventual
destruction.

My work also extended into the transformation of practical locations and
objects through scenic ageing, signage, vinyl applications and lighting
details, helping establish the film's American setting while shooting in Cape
Town. This included scenic work on vehicles and road signage, installing
architectural LED details, and painting a caravan for a desert sequence that
would ultimately be destroyed on camera.

The production was an opportunity to experience another side of world-building
for film, creating familiar, contemporary spaces while considering character,
narrative, camera and stunt requirements simultaneously.
