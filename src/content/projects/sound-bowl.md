Sound Bowl is an interactive installation exploring the relationship between
sound, vibration, water and movement. Developed under Passages, a collaborative
creative practice with my partner, the work transforms sound frequencies into
visible cymatic patterns across the surface of a large shallow vessel filled
with water.

Activated at Tomorrow's Monday, the installation became a gathering point.
People surrounded the bowl, experimented with different frequencies and
interacted directly with the water as vibration formed temporary patterns,
ripples and shifting geometries across its surface.

Part experiment, part sensory experience, Sound Bowl makes sound physical and
visible, inviting people to observe, touch and play with sound made visible,
something usually experienced only through listening. The work explores how a
simple meeting of sound and material can become a shared, immersive,
participatory experience.
