      Shot amongst the colourful streets and architecture of Bo-Kaap, Cape
      Town, Womanhood explores femininity, sisterhood and collective identity

    title: Still Life
    body: |-
      This project was my first opportunity to explore still-life styling
      within film, working between object composition, miniature set building
      and styling directly onto the body.

      I created small, sculptural worlds using foraged vines, branches, flowers
      and natural forms alongside stone, glass, jewellery, found objects and
      sourced pieces. Compositions were assembled on granite and marble
      surfaces, playing with balance, scale, texture and unexpected
      relationships between objects before being brought into the final shots.

      The process extended from tabletop still lifes onto the models
      themselves, where plants, stones and organic forms were balanced, wrapped
      and positioned around the body, blurring the boundary between still life,
      sculpture and styling.

This ongoing collaboration with photographer [Nadia Von
Scotti](https://www.nadiavonscotti.com/) developed from a shared interest in
creating image-led worlds through photography, location, objects and styling.
Working from Nadia's initial concepts, moodboards and casting, I was brought on
to develop the set decoration and prop styling, with the creative freedom to
interpret each brief through my own visual practice.

Together we approached each shoot as an opportunity for experimentation,
responding to location, colour, texture and found objects to build environments
around the subjects rather than simply dressing a background. The collaboration
became a meeting point between Nadia's photographic language and my own
approach to spatial composition, material and visual storytelling.
