Over the last two editions, I have been given the opportunity to design and
fabricate a lamp for [The Nest](/projects/the-nest/) each year, extending the
material language of the wider space into individual lighting pieces.

Each lamp is developed through experimentation with materials from the land and
repurposed textiles — sheep's wool, chicken wire, hessian, twine, thread and
scrap fabrics naturally dyed with turmeric, rust and iron, alongside botanical
eco-printing.
