I have been creating with Pangea the Tribe since 2019, a collaboration that has
played a significant role in shaping my creative practice. My involvement has
evolved from décor, signage and fabrication to the collaborative creative
direction, project management and stage design of what is now the **Enigma**
stage.

What continues to draw me to this work is the process of building a temporary
world from the ground up. I work across concept development, design and
fabrication, translating an initial vision into a fully realised eclectic,
quirky environment through architecture, scenic elements, spatial details and
visual storytelling.
