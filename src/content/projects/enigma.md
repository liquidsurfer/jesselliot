I have been creating with Pangea the Tribe since 2019, a collaboration that has
played a significant role in shaping my creative practice. My involvement has
evolved from décor, signage and fabrication to the collaborative creative
direction, project management and stage design of what is now the **Enigma**
stage.

Enigma is built with love as a temporary world of its own. Curious
interventions transforms the dance floor into an environment that feels
playful, surreal and deliberately unexpected.

My work here moves between concept development, spatial design and hands on
making, translating the wider visual language of Enigma into architecture,
scenic treatments, fabricated details and the smaller discoveries hidden
throughout the space. Rather than treating décor as something added afterwards,
each element becomes part of the world itself, giving the stage it's character
and creating moments of curiosity, humour and discovery as people move through
it.
