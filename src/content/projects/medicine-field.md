I had the opportunity to lead the fabrication and execution of the Ring & Booth
for Kunda Valley's new main stage **The Medicine Field**. In designing and
collaborating with the Kunda founders, my role spanned concept development and
stage design, including the central ring structure, DJ booth façade, tower
windows, top dressing and scenic treatments.

What drew me to Kunda Valley was its grounded relationship with the land and
the natural environment. The design developed from this ethos, combining
minimal architectural forms with a tactile material language of steel, bendy
ply, polycarbonate, layered with textural materials, fabrics, scenic finishes
and natural elements. At its centre, the vinyl-focused DJ booth became both a
functional and defining feature of the space for the first iteration of the
main stage.
