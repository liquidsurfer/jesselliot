In collaboration with the Kunda founders, I had the opportunity to lead the
collaborative design of **The Medicine Field**, Kunda Valley's new main stage.
My role spanned concept development and stage design, including the central ring
structure, DJ booth façade, tower windows, top dressing and scenic treatments.

What drew me to Kunda Valley was its grounded relationship with the land and the
natural environment. The design developed from this ethos, combining minimal
architectural forms with a tactile material language of steel, bendy ply,
hessian and polycarbonate, layered with scenic finishes and natural elements.

At its centre, the vinyl-focused DJ booth became both a functional and defining
feature of the space.
